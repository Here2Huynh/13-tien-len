    13 — Tiến Lên

    You vs Mina · Kenji · Rosa

    First to 13 wins the match.

    How to play

    GOAL

    First to 13 points. Shed all your cards, finish high, and don't get caught holding the big stuff.

    START

    The 3♠ holder leads the first trick of the match, and the 3♠ must be in that play.

    YOUR TURN

    Beat the play on the table with the same kind of set, higher — or pass. Passing locks you out of the trick. The trick winner leads next.

    CARDS

    3 is low, 2 is high; suits run ♠ < ♣ < ♦ < ♥, so 2♥ is the biggest card in the game. Sets: singles, pairs, triples, four of a kind, straights, and pair runs.

    BOMBS BEAT 2s

    A 3-pair run bombs a single 2; a 4-pair run bombs a pair of 2s; four of a kind bombs either. Already passed? Only four of a kind and the 4-pair run can bring you back in.

    BOMB PENALTY

    Bomb a 2 and its owner pays you 1 point per 2 — instantly.

    INSTANT WINS (dealt hands)

    Four 2s · six pairs · five straight pairs · dragon straight 3–A · three straight triples. Everyone pays you 2 and the round ends.

    SCORING

    1st +2, 2nd +1, 3rd pays 1 to 2nd, last pays 2 to 1st. Still holding 2s or unused bombs when the round ends: 1 point each, paid to the player above you.
